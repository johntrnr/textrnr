frequencr

web · soon

about

a camera that redraws whatever it sees as frequency-modulated lines: neon oscilloscope waves that bend around hands, faces and bodies. it runs live on the camera feed or on photos from your library, with a deep set of controls to push the lines from clean signal to organic, textured noise.

coming soon

features

  1. live camera, rendered in real time with webgl shaders
  2. import from your photo library
  3. frequency-modulation line rendering, neon on black
  4. lots of controls to shape the waves, from clean to organic and textured
  5. filter / preset switcher
  6. photo and video export
  7. mobile-first camera interface: rounded viewfinder, vertical sliders, live log
