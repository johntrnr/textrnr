phasr

web · soon

about

a generative raster studio for dithering, ascii and pixel art. 105 effects, from classic dithering and halftone to generative systems, glitch and the exact look of 43 retro consoles, stacked as layers and exported at up to 16× resolution.

coming soon

features

  1. 105 effects in six families: pixel, systems, line, texture, glitch, retro consoles
  2. dithering, ascii, halftone, stippling, risograph, threshold, pixel art
  3. 43 retro console renderers: game boy, nes, snes, c64, amiga, ps1, n64, pico-8…
  4. generative systems: reaction diffusion, flow fields, lorenz attractors, boids, lenia
  5. glitch: rgb shift, crt scanlines, pixel sorting, vhs noise, databend
  6. multi-layer compositing with blend modes
  7. duo, tri and quad tone colour matrix, custom palettes, random colour schemes
  8. image adjustments, post-processing, borders and mattes
  9. presets
  10. batch processing
  11. export png, jpeg and svg at up to 16× resolution
  12. mobile layout with bottom sheets
