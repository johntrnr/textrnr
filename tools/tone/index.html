tone

app · soon

about

turns photos into halftone overprints: overlapping dots and ink in one to three colours, like a two-colour poster or a riso-printed zine. it can pick the inks that suit the photo for you, or you set every screen by hand.

coming soon

features

  1. 1 to 3 colour overprint
  2. automatic ink selection matched to the photo, or manual
  3. dot fineness, screen angle and per-colour intensity
  4. two screening methods: am dots and grain
  5. three paper whites: warm, natural, white
  6. margin, aspect ratio and corner radius
  7. step-by-step pipeline: orig, lin, tone, gamut, separation … composite
  8. saveable presets, including your own
  9. png export for sharing and print
  10. v3ktrz ink unlock: 7 extra inks, custom preset storage, high-res export
