tex — textrnr

tex is a belgian hybrid artist working between photography, ai and code.

he started making ai art in 2022 with disco diffusion, and has since released 1/1s and editions on superrare, foundation, transient labs, objkt and ninfa. the work is quiet and atmospheric: buildings, homes and ordinary neighbourhoods at different hours of the day, and lately blurred night photography in neon reds and blues.

alongside the art he designs and builds his own creative tools — cameras, dithering and halftone studios, upscalers, editors — collected on this site.
