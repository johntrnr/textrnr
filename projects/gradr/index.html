gradr

web · soon

about

a minimal gradient studio for abstract backgrounds and wallpapers. build linear, radial, conic or mesh gradients, add grain and finish, and export at the exact size of your phone, laptop or post.

coming soon

features

  1. linear, radial, conic and mesh gradients
  2. colour stops with opacity, draggable mesh nodes
  3. smoothing modes: linear, smooth, cosine, cubic, oklab
  4. grain, vignette, blur and anti-banding dither
  5. brightness, contrast, saturation and hue
  6. colour harmony generator: analogous, complementary, triadic…
  7. 24 presets: minimal, vibrant, dark, pastel, aura
  8. 16 sizes: iphone, galaxy, pixel, ipad, macbook, 4k, story, x and youtube banners
  9. export png, jpeg, webp, svg and css
  10. installable pwa that works offline
